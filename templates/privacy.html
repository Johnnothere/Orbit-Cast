Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated 7 August 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what OrbitCastAI collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies whenever you use the site at this domain.

1. What we collect, and only with your OK

Nothing described below is collected unless you accept the banner shown on your first visit. Declining means none of this is stored — the app still works, matching you to events in that single session, but nothing persists after you leave.

If you accept, we keep:

  • Your CV or resume, as submitted (the original file, plus the text OrbitCastAI extracted from it).
  • Your extracted profile — the honest read of strengths, gaps, and trajectory OrbitCastAI generated from what you gave us. On a later visit, this lets you reuse your last profile instead of uploading again — you always get an explicit prompt for this, with the option to start fresh instead.
  • Which recommendations you were shown, and which you clicked through to — used only to calibrate the matching engine over time.
  • Your location, if you separately allow it — used only to show distance and a map on recommendations. This never changes which events are recommended to you.
  • Where you came from — the referring page and any utm_ campaign parameters present on your first visit, captured once, at the moment you accept. Used only in our own aggregate reporting (e.g. "how many people who arrive from X go on to submit a CV") — never at an individual level, never shared outside OrbitCastAI, and never used to track you on any other site.

2. Cookies

We set one cookie (oc_uid) so your browser is recognised between visits. It's a random identifier — not your name, email, or any other identity. It is not used for advertising and is not shared with any ad network or data broker.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with any third party, and we don't run third-party ad tracking or analytics scripts on this site.
  • We don't use your CV, profile, location, or traffic-source data for anything beyond running and improving OrbitCastAI's own matching engine and understanding our own traffic.
  • We don't require an account, email, or real-name identity to use the service.
  • We don't use device fingerprinting, and we have no access to — and never collect — your device's MAC address or hardware identifiers. If you decline or clear your cookie, we have no way to recognise you again; that's by design, not an oversight.

4. How matching works

OrbitCastAI reads your CV or self-description, scores it honestly against a live catalog of events, and shows only the ones that genuinely fit — often none. Recommendations are ranked by fit, not by anything you pay for or anyone else's promotion.

5. Your rights

You can request deletion of everything tied to your browser at any time — no reason needed, no account required. This permanently erases your stored CV/resume, profile, analyses, recommendations, click history, and location, and cannot be undone.

6. Changes to this policy

If what we collect or why changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will change and, where the change is significant, the consent banner will be shown again.
